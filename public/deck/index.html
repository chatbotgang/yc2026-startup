偏鄉長者Rural elders

例如：華山基金會e.g. Hwa Shan Foundation

痛點Problem

長輩分不出詐騙、看不懂通知，社工一個人顧幾十位。Elders can't spot scams or read notices; one caseworker covers dozens.

Agent 怎麼解Agent solution

24 小時接長輩的問題，判斷詐騙、讀懂通知，異常立刻通知社工。Answers elders 24/7, flags scams, explains notices, alerts the caseworker.

流浪動物Stray animals

例如：台灣動物緊急救援小組e.g. Animal Rescue Team Taiwan

痛點Problem

約 14 萬隻流浪狗，通報和認養詢問塞爆志工的手機。About 141,500 stray dogs; reports and adoption questions flood volunteers.

Agent 怎麼解Agent solution

自動分流救援通報、回答認養問題、排志工班，人力留給現場。Triages reports, answers adoption questions, schedules volunteers.

移工翻譯Migrant workers

例如：One-Forty 四十分之一e.g. One-Forty

痛點Problem

近 90 萬移工，語言不通，看不懂契約、找不到求助管道。Nearly 900,000 migrant workers who can't read contracts or find help.

Agent 怎麼解Agent solution

用母語解釋契約與就醫流程，法律或安全問題轉給社工。Explains contracts and clinics in their language; hands legal or safety issues to staff.
