我們能做什麼What we can do

Share the Speed.

把你的 AI 算力，分一份給服務弱勢的 NGO。Share a slice of your AI capacity with the NGOs that serve people left behind.

同一份算力，幫到三種被留下的人。One pool of compute. Three groups no longer left behind.

偏鄉長者Rural elders
華

例如：華山基金會e.g. Hwa Shan Foundation

各地「天使站」照顧失能、失智、失依的長輩。agent 幫長輩判斷詐騙電話、讀懂補助通知，異常時通知社工。Local "angel stations" care for frail elders. The agent screens scam calls, reads subsidy notices, and alerts a caseworker when something is wrong.

流浪動物Stray animals

例如：台灣動物緊急救援小組e.g. Animal Rescue Team Taiwan

台灣仍有約 14 萬隻流浪狗。agent 接手救援通報分流、認養問答與志工排班，讓人力留給現場。Taiwan still has about 141,500 stray dogs. The agent triages rescue reports, answers adoption questions and schedules volunteers.

移工翻譯Migrant workers

例如：One-Forty 四十分之一e.g. One-Forty

台灣有近 90 萬移工。agent 用母語解釋契約、就醫與求助管道，法律或安全問題轉給社工。Taiwan has nearly 900,000 migrant workers. The agent explains contracts, clinics and help lines in their language, and hands legal or safety issues to staff.
